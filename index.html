FAQ

Good to know before you book.

How does pickup and drop-off work?

It's arranged individually for each rental — message us with your dates and we'll figure out what works for your move. Pickup and drop-off options are most flexible within Peterborough itself.

What if a tote gets lost or damaged?

Normal wear and tear isn't charged. Lost or seriously damaged totes are charged per a fixed fee schedule, and we'll always send photo documentation and an explanation before charging anything.

Is there a deposit?

Yes — a refundable $100 CAD security hold is authorized on your card at booking. It's only charged against if totes come back lost, damaged, or late; otherwise it's simply released.

How long can I keep the totes?

Rentals run one week by default. Need more time? Extra weeks are $1.50 per tote.

Do you have more than 40 totes available?

Yes — custom, larger orders (up to 60 totes) are available on request, subject to availability. Get in touch and we'll confirm what we can do for your move date.
